# Legal and rights risk review — 2026-10-30

## Status and scope

This is a preliminary risk inventory for the existing 11 educational repositories, not a legal opinion or a certification of compliance. The current review can inspect public source code and content, but cannot establish the operator's legal identity, applicable municipality/prefecture, actual hosting configuration, all third-party agreements, user-data flows, or the provenance/licences of every source text and image.

## Required controls

- Prefer original question wording, original example data, and original diagrams. Do not copy complete exam questions, textbook passages, commercial workbook items, official answer explanations, or images without a documented legal basis and permission where required.
- For any quoted/reused work, keep a provenance record: source, author/rights holder, URL, retrieval date, exact scope, applicable licence/permission, required attribution, and rationale for any statutory exception. A generic “source” note is not by itself proof of permission.
- Review all personal-data collection, analytics, browser storage, external requests, logs, retention, deletion, and third-party transfers. Do not request or publish student names, addresses, phone numbers, school IDs, or other identifiable information unless necessary and lawfully handled.
- Keep generated data clearly distinguished from real-world data. Synthetic statistics, fabricated case studies, and hypothetical legal scenarios must not be presented as official records or actual measurements.
- Constitutional and public-law statements should be framed accurately and neutrally; distinguish text of the Constitution, legislation, judicial holdings, government interpretation, and editorial opinion. Avoid presenting contested interpretations as settled fact.
- Determine which education-related laws, local ordinances, school policies, and consumer/online service terms apply only after identifying the operator, audience, service model, and jurisdictions. A code-only scan cannot make this determination.
- Publish clear terms of use, privacy information, contact/rights-request route, attribution and correction process, and any limitations on educational use that are actually applicable.

## Open items that prevent a full compliance conclusion

1. No complete rights/provenance register for every question, passage, figure, table, image, and explanation has been independently verified.
2. No exhaustive review of all 11 repositories' external data flows and production configuration has been completed.
3. The operator's identity, applicable local ordinances, school/municipal rules, and all service-specific terms have not been established.
4. Every constitutional/legal assertion has not been independently checked against primary sources and dated for amendments/current status.
5. Privacy notice, retention/deletion, access controls, incident response, and any vendor contracts require operator-level confirmation.

## Gate

Do not automatically publish any item with unclear provenance, personal data, a potentially misleading factual claim, or an unresolved legal/constitutional assertion. Flag it for human review and keep it out of automated write paths. A green structural test does not imply legal clearance.
